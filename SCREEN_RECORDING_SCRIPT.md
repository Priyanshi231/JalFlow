# JalFlow – 60–75 Second Screen Recording Script

1. Open the JalFlow Dashboard.
   - Briefly show Total Bookings, Active Deliveries, Revenue and Available Drivers.

2. Open Bookings.
   - Click "New Booking".
   - Enter a customer, address, quantity and amount.
   - Create the booking.
   - Show the new booking in the table.

3. Assign a driver.
   - Use the Driver column.
   - Select a driver.
   - Change the booking status to "On the Way".

4. Open Payments.
   - Click "Record Payment".
   - Select the booking.
   - Save a paid payment.
   - Show it in Payment History.

5. Open Reports.
   - Show Booking Trend, Revenue Trend and Top Customers.

6. End on the dashboard.

Keep the recording between 30 and 90 seconds.
